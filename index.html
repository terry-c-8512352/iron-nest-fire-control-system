⏱ Time-of-flight calibration

Flight times now come from a measured table — timed in game, four shots per charge. Time is proportional to range for a given charge: C1 4.81 · C2 3.87 · C3 2.61 · C4 1.90 · C5 1.54 · C6 1.43 s/km. Nothing here needs calibrating unless the game changes.

To override a charge with your own timings, log charge · range · seconds below. Two or more points for a charge replace the built-in figure with your own fit; a single point is kept but not used, since the table is fitted from four samples.
